# Density Dimension

Information density per slide.

## Options

### minimal
- **Content**: 1 key point per slide maximum
- **Whitespace**: 50%+ of slide area
- **Elements**: 2-3 visual elements max
- **Text Lines**: Headlines + 1-2 short lines
- **Best For**: Executives, keynotes, emotional impact
- **Principle**: Maximum breathing room, one focal point

### balanced
- **Content**: 2-3 key points per slide
- **Whitespace**: 30-40% of slide area
- **Elements**: 3-5 visual elements
- **Text Lines**: Headlines + 3-5 bullet points or equivalent
- **Best For**: General audience, standard presentations
- **Principle**: Information with clarity, structured layout

### dense
- **Content**: 3-5 key points per slide
- **Whitespace**: 15-25% of slide area
- **Elements**: 5-8 visual elements
- **Text Lines**: Headlines + multiple sections with bullets
- **Best For**: Experts, technical reviews, data-heavy
- **Principle**: Every element earns its space, data over decoration
