# Mood Dimension

Color temperature and palette.

## Options

### professional
- **Feel**: Business credibility, institutional
- **Palette**:
  - Background: Pure White (#FFFFFF)
  - Primary Text: Navy (#1E3A5F)
  - Secondary Text: Dark Gray (#4A5568)
  - Accent 1: Gold (#C9A227) - premium highlights
  - Accent 2: Light Navy (#3D5A80)

### warm
- **Feel**: Approachable, friendly, educational
- **Palette**:
  - Background: Cream (#FDF6E3)
  - Primary Text: Warm Brown (#5D4037)
  - Secondary Text: Medium Brown (#795548)
  - Accent 1: Coral (#FF6B6B) - highlights
  - Accent 2: Amber (#FFB74D)

### cool
- **Feel**: Analytical, precise, technical
- **Palette**:
  - Background: Cool White (#F8FAFC)
  - Primary Text: Dark Blue (#1A365D)
  - Secondary Text: Slate (#475569)
  - Accent 1: Cyan (#0EA5E9) - highlights
  - Accent 2: Teal (#14B8A6)

### vibrant
- **Feel**: Bold, energetic, modern
- **Palette**:
  - Background: White (#FFFFFF) or Dark (#1A1A2E)
  - Primary Text: Deep Charcoal (#2D2D2D) or White
  - Accent 1: Electric Blue (#3B82F6)
  - Accent 2: Hot Pink (#EC4899)
  - Accent 3: Lime (#84CC16)

### dark
- **Feel**: Cinematic, dramatic, immersive
- **Palette**:
  - Background: Deep Black (#0F0F1A)
  - Primary Text: White (#F8F8F8)
  - Secondary Text: Light Gray (#A0AEC0)
  - Accent 1: Neon Blue (#60A5FA) - glowing
  - Accent 2: Electric Purple (#A78BFA)

### neutral
- **Feel**: Minimal, sophisticated, calm
- **Palette**:
  - Background: Off White (#FAFAFA)
  - Primary Text: Near Black (#1A1A1A)
  - Secondary Text: Medium Gray (#6B7280)
  - Accent 1: Charcoal (#374151) - subtle emphasis
  - Accent 2: Light Gray (#D1D5DB)
