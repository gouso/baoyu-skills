# Typography Dimension

Font style and text treatment.

**Important**: Describe visual appearance for image generation, NOT font names.

## Options

### geometric
- **Headlines**: Bold geometric sans-serif with perfect circular O shapes, even stroke width, modern and clean. Medium to semi-bold weight.
- **Body**: Light geometric sans-serif, highly legible, uniform letter spacing.
- **Feel**: Modern, corporate, tech
- **Best For**: Business, SaaS, product

### humanist
- **Headlines**: Rounded sans-serif with warm curves, varying stroke width, friendly and approachable. Semi-bold weight.
- **Body**: Readable humanist sans-serif, open apertures, comfortable for long reading.
- **Feel**: Friendly, warm, accessible
- **Best For**: Education, lifestyle, community

### handwritten
- **Headlines**: Hand-lettered style with natural stroke variation, organic imperfections, marker or brush feel. Bold weight.
- **Body**: Casual handwriting style, readable but organic, slight slant.
- **Feel**: Personal, creative, informal
- **Best For**: Tutorials, storytelling, creative

### editorial
- **Headlines**: High-contrast serif with dramatic thick/thin strokes, magazine-style impact. Bold weight with tight tracking.
- **Body**: Classic serif with elegant curves, optimized for readability, traditional publishing quality.
- **Feel**: Authoritative, dramatic, premium
- **Best For**: Keynotes, launches, editorial

### technical
- **Headlines**: Monospace or semi-monospace with even character width, engineering aesthetic. Medium weight.
- **Body**: Clean monospace, code-editor readability, precise alignment.
- **Feel**: Precise, systematic, data-driven
- **Best For**: Technical docs, architecture, code
